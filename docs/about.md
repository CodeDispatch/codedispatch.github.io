# About us
## What is CodeDispatch?
CodeDispatch is a free online "Newspaper" for developers, shipping high-quality articles, reviews, guides and more to help other people out, while staying in a non-political position to enhance the quality and experience of everyone.

## The Founder
CodeDispatch was founded by [Intensed-Dev](https://github.com/intensed-dev) to showcase and guide users and developers through news, products and software.

## Our Missions
1. Stay in a non-political position
2. Use trustworthy sources and people for our articles
