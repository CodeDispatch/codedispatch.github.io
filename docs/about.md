# About CodeDispatch

## What is CodeDispatch?

**CodeDispatch** is a free, independent online publication focused on technology, software, and development.

We publish **news, reviews, guides, explainers, and technical coverage** designed to help developers, creators, and technology enthusiasts stay informed and make better decisions.

Our goal is simple: **make technology easier to understand and keep our reporting useful, accessible, and trustworthy.**

CodeDispatch is not affiliated with any political party or political organization. We aim to keep our coverage focused on technology rather than political agendas, allowing readers from different backgrounds to engage with our content.

## Our Editorial Standards

We believe good technology journalism starts with reliable information.

When researching and writing our articles, we aim to:

1. **Use trustworthy sources** and verify important claims whenever possible.
2. **Distinguish facts from interpretation** and avoid presenting speculation as fact.
3. **Provide context**, rather than simply reporting headlines.
4. **Remain independent** and avoid political or ideological agendas.
5. **Correct mistakes** when inaccurate information is identified.
6. **Write for everyone**, from people discovering a technology for the first time to experienced developers.

## What We Cover

CodeDispatch focuses primarily on:

- **Software & Development** — programming languages, frameworks, tools, APIs, and developer platforms.
- **Technology News** — relevant developments across the software and technology industry.
- **Reviews** — software, services, developer tools, and other technology products.
- **Guides & Tutorials** — practical explanations designed to help readers learn and solve problems.
- **Open Source** — projects, communities, releases, and developments within the open-source ecosystem.
- **Industry & Platforms** — major companies, platforms, and changes that affect developers and technology users.

## The Founder

CodeDispatch was founded by **[Intensed-Dev](https://github.com/intensed-dev)** as an independent project focused on making technology news and information more accessible to developers and enthusiasts.

The project was created around a simple idea: **technology coverage should be understandable, useful, and backed by reliable information.**

## Our Mission

Our mission is to build a reliable and accessible source for technology and software coverage.

We strive to:

1. **Inform** readers about relevant developments in technology.
2. **Explain** complex technical topics in an understandable way.
3. **Review** products and software fairly and transparently.
4. **Support** developers and creators with useful resources and guides.
5. **Maintain editorial independence** and avoid political agendas.
6. **Build trust** through responsible research, sourcing, and corrections.

## Transparency

CodeDispatch is a free publication. We aim to be transparent about our sources, methodology, and potential conflicts of interest.

When an article relies on external information, we make an effort to provide appropriate sources so readers can verify the information themselves.

CodeDispatch may evolve over time, but our core principle remains the same:

> **Useful technology coverage, without the noise.**
